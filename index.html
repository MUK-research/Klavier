About the descriptors

Velocity is MIDI key velocity, not acoustic loudness. Pace is a score-free proxy obtained from the median interval between distinct onset groups, so it should not be interpreted as a definitive beat-tracking estimate. Articulation is a score-free duration/next-attack ratio. These intentionally remain interpretable measurements rather than a single judgement of “good phrasing.”
